Privacy Policy — Amar Bangladesh News Cards

This tool is used only by the owner of the "Amar Bangladesh" Facebook Page (amarbangladesh.site) to turn news links into photo cards and publish them on that Page after the owner approves each one.

What data it uses

It stores the news links the owner enters, the text and card images it prepares, and the Page access token needed to publish to the owner's own Page. It does not collect any data from Facebook users, visitors or followers, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

Data deletion

Posted items are removed automatically after 14 days. To delete everything, the owner can remove the app's access from Facebook settings or contact us at amarbangladesh.site.
